# Privacy Policy for Uktam.ai

**Effective Date: August 2026**

Uktam.ai ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, use, and disclosure practices.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Uktam.ai is a privacy-first application. While all core speech pipeline features operate entirely offline, we do collect limited, anonymized performance data to improve the application (detailed in Section 2).

### Audio Data and Microphone Access
Our application requires access to your device's microphone (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`) to provide real-time speech-to-text functionality.
- All audio processing and speech recognition is performed **locally on your device**.
- Your voice recordings are **never** transmitted over the internet, sent to any cloud servers, or saved to your device's permanent storage.
- The transcribed text and translations are processed solely on your device using local AI models and are not collected by us or any third parties.

## 2. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Third-Party Services)

To help us identify bugs, optimize performance, and improve the application, we use **Google Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics**.

These services automatically collect limited, anonymized telemetry data when you use the app, including:
- **Device Information:** Non-identifying device IDs, operating system version, and device model.
- **Performance Metrics:** Data on how long certain tasks take (e.g., translation speeds, model loading times).
- **Crash Data:** Diagnostic information and stack traces if the application crashes.

**Important:** These third-party analytics services **do not** have access to your microphone, your spoken audio, or your translated text. The data collected is strictly limited to application performance and stability. You can view Google’s Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 3. Data Security

Because all processing happens on your device, your data is inherently secure. Your speech and translated text never leave your smartphone.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates to this policy will be reflected in this document and on our repository.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by opening an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Uktam-ai/uktam).
